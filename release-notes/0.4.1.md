## Trader generation and lifecycle

- Administrators can create a configured NPC trader through `/shop createtrader`, `/shop ct`, or the Shop radial menu.
- Generated traders receive a localized name, weighted skin colors, configurable outfits, and safe interaction flags.
- Trader settings now offer a confirmed dissolution that settles stock and balances into the world account before archival.
- Deleted or killed registered trader NPCs are automatically dissolved when Shop enables, so their Wallet and stock records cannot remain orphaned.
- Dummy-NPC hairstyles are deferred pending an upstream Rising World API/renderer fix.
