## In-game offer editor

- Administrators can create a missing custom offer file directly from Zone or Trader settings after confirmation.
- Custom Zone and Trader offer files can be maintained in game: add an item from the complete catalog, edit stock/economy settings, or remove an offer safely.
- Trader offer removal settles remaining stock to the Trader account before clearing that trader's scoped stock.
- System-offer cards retain the regular unit sell price even when the player has none of that item in inventory.
