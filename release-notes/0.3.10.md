## Concrete system-shop items

- System-shop clothing offers now use concrete item definitions and matching
  icons instead of the generic `clothingitem` placeholder.
- Supported workbench clothing recipes are included in the generated catalog.
- Construction offers now use concrete definitions, texture variants, and
  matching icons instead of the generic `constructionitem` placeholder.
- Recipe-backed construction variants are included in the complete catalog and
  generated default offers.
