# OZ Shop 0.13.0

- Leaders and officers can create male or female faction traders within faction capacity. Native dialogs confirm placement and dissolution; trader headers identify the owning faction.
- Faction traders use individual faction offer files and the faction Wallet account. Their stock is player supplied; stock limits can be edited, while target stock stays zero and automatic stock movement is disabled.
- Trades reserve stock and use idempotent faction account settlement with compensation. Empty stock, missing funds or missing account data block trade. Removing an offer or dissolving a trader deletes its remaining stock without world-account liquidation.
- Existing personal and global traders retain their behavior.

Update OZ - Factions to 0.5.0 before enabling faction traders. Back up the Shop database and faction offer files before updating or rolling back. Remove faction traders and resolve open trade before restoring older plugin versions.

Requires Java 25, Rising World PluginAPI 0.9.3.2 and OZ Tools 0.28.0.
