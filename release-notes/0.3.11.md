## Plugin purchase feedback and zone access

- Plugin-provided offers now show a purchase confirmation by default.
- Players can individually disable the confirmation or the success message in
  their OZ Shop settings.
- Administrators can open the Zone tab while shop zones are required; it is
  clearly presented as an administration tab.
- The shop-zone setting description now fits the administrator settings UI.

The new confirmation and success-message settings are additive player
preferences. Existing shop offers, zones, stock, and settings remain compatible.
